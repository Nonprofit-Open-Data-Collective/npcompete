# npcompete: Competition and Density Metrics for Nonprofit Markets

Builds measures of competition in local nonprofit markets, including the
Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, normalized HHI, concentration ratios, the
Kwoka index, Gini coefficients and population-adjusted density, by
geography, subsector and year.
